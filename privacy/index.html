Privacy Notice

This notice explains how CRUVIT handles information for the Premium Garden Intelligence early-access validation (willingness-to-pay test). It is a plain-language transparency notice for this validation — not a claim of certification or full compliance with every privacy law worldwide.

Who

CRUVIT operates this validation. Contact: office@cruvit.com.

What we collect

For this Premium early-access validation, we may collect:

  • email address, when you voluntarily submit it
  • your explicit consent to receive CRUVIT Premium early-access communication
  • page/test interactions such as offer view, Premium CTA click, and early-access completion
  • source or campaign identifier, if present in the link you used
  • timestamp
  • WTP test price shown (currently $7.99 / month as a test price only)
  • limited session/test metadata needed to measure the experiment

We do not collect payment card details through this validation.

Why

We use this information only to:

  • measure demand for CRUVIT Premium Garden Intelligence
  • operate the Premium early-access validation
  • contact people who explicitly join the Premium early-access list

We do not enroll you in unrelated marketing based on this consent.

Payment

No payment information is collected. No card information is collected. No payment is taken in this validation test.

Service providers

CRUVIT uses hosting and infrastructure providers needed to operate the site and this validation, including Netlify where applicable. Participant emails from this validation are not sold.

Selling data

CRUVIT does not sell participant email addresses collected through this validation test.

Retention

Personal data from this validation is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to evaluate the WTP validation, administer Premium early access, and meet legitimate operational or legal needs. When it is no longer needed, it should be deleted or de-identified. Aggregate, non-identifying validation results may be retained for business analysis.

Your requests

You may contact office@cruvit.com to request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your early-access submission, subject to applicable law and reasonable identity verification.
